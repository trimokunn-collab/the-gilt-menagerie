Tonight’s job

The Gilt Menagerie

A private menagerie of jewelled animals, locked up for the night. Cross the galleries, lift what you can carry, and leave by the south door — the door only opens once your bag holds $40,000 of treasure.

  • Move — WASD or arrow keys
  • Sneak — hold Shift: slower, quieter, harder to spot
  • Steal — walk onto a glowing treasure
  • Hide — walls, cabinets and cases block sight
  • Restart — R  ·  Sound — M

Guard lanterns and camera eyes fill the alarm. A full alarm ends the night.

Clean getaway

Out into the night

The south door sighs shut behind you. Somewhere across the city, a fence is about to have a very good morning.

Guard lanterns sweep as they patrol — a lit thief fills the alarm fast. Cameras blink red while you are in their eye. The Star Crown in the vault is worth $40,000 alone, if you dare.
